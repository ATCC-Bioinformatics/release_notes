### Cell Biology Releases
This Q3 2026 release marks the fourth bulk release of authenticated cell biology datasets available through the ATCC Genome Portal.

The release includes **51 new RNA-seq datasets** spanning both newly published Cell Biology pages and updates to previously released models. Specifically:

- **43 new cell biology pages** were added to the ATCC Genome Portal, representing new human cell-line models and associated RNA-seq datasets.

- **8 existing cell biology pages** received newly generated RNA-seq datasets, including **5 human cell line models** and **3 patient-derived organoid models**.

Updated existing models include:

###### **Human cell lines**

- U-87 MG-Luc2 (ATCC® HTB-14-LUC2™)
- SFT (ATCC® CRL-3469™)
- HCT 116 (ATCC® CCL-247™)
- HepatoXcell™ Plus: Normal Human Hepatocytes (ATCC® PCS-450-010™)
- NIH:OVCAR-3 (ATCC® HTB-161™)

###### **Patient-derived organoids**

- HCM-CSHL-0155-C50 (ATCC® PDM-92™)
- HCM-CSHL-0773-C50 (ATCC® PDM-523™)
- HCM-CSHL-0655-C50 (ATCC® PDM-411™)
